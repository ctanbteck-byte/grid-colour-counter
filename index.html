How It Works
①Upload or capture your 32×32 numbered-colour grid
②Pass 1 — OCR reads all 1,024 cells row by row
③Pass 2 — Independent verification scan, flags discrepancies
④Reconciliation merges both reads; checksum verifies 1,024 cells
⑤Results show count & % per colour with confidence rating
